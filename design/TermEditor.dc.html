## Definition The equilibrium constant for loss of a proton from a surface hydroxyl group, $$ \equiv\!\mathrm{SOH} \rightleftharpoons \equiv\!\mathrm{SO}^- + \mathrm{H}^+, \qquad K_a = \frac{[\equiv\!\mathrm{SO}^-]\,a_{\mathrm{H}^+}}{[\equiv\!\mathrm{SOH}]} $$ usually quoted as $\mathrm{p}K_a = -\log_{10} K_a$. Together with the protonation constant it sets the point of zero charge of an oxide surface. ## Notes Definition drafted when the vault was set up; check it against your source.

surface pKa

Definition

The equilibrium constant for loss of a proton from a surface hydroxyl group,

≡SOH ⇌ ≡SO− + H+,    Ka = [≡SO−] aH+[≡SOH]

usually quoted as pKa = −log10 Ka. Together with the protonation constant it sets the point of zero charge of an oxide surface.
