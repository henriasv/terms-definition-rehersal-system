# Salicylic acid

## Definition

A phenolic acid, $\mathrm{C_7H_6O_3}$, with a carboxylic acid ortho to a hydroxyl group on a benzene ring. Precursor of aspirin.

```smiles
CC(=O)Oc1ccccc1C(=O)O
```

## Notes

The intramolecular hydrogen bond lowers the first $\mathrm{p}K_a$ to about 2.97.
