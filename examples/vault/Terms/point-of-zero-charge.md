# Point of zero charge

## Definition

The pH at which the net surface charge of a solid in contact with an electrolyte is zero. For a surface with one protonation and one deprotonation step,

$$ "pH"_"PZC" = 1/2 (p K_(a 1) + p K_(a 2)) $$

where $p K_(a 1)$ and $p K_(a 2)$ are the [[Surface deprotonation constant|surface pKa]] values.
