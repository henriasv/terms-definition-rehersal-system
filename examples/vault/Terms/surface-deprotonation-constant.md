# Surface deprotonation constant

## Definition

The equilibrium constant for loss of a proton from a surface hydroxyl group,

$$
\equiv\!\mathrm{SOH} \rightleftharpoons \equiv\!\mathrm{SO}^- + \mathrm{H}^+,
\qquad K_a = \frac{[\equiv\!\mathrm{SO}^-]\,a_{\mathrm{H}^+}}{[\equiv\!\mathrm{SOH}]}
$$

usually quoted as $\mathrm{p}K_a = -\log_{10} K_a$. Together with the protonation constant it sets the point of zero charge of an oxide surface.
